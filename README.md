# Lebanon's Consumer Price Crisis

An interactive Streamlit dashboard exploring consumer price trends and food inflation in Lebanon from 2001 to 2023.

## About the app

The dashboard uses Lebanon Consumer Price Index data from FAO / HumData.

It contains two related visualizations:

- Overall Food Price Index and General Price Index trends over time
- Monthly food inflation for a selected year

The app includes two linked interaction features. The user first selects a year range, then selects a specific year from within that range to examine monthly food inflation in more detail.
